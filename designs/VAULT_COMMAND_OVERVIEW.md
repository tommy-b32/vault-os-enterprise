# Vault Command

## Purpose

Vault Command is the operational headquarters of Vault OS.

It is not an analytics dashboard.

It is an operating centre designed to help business owners understand what is happening inside their store, why it is happening and what action should be taken next.

The first deployment of Vault Command is for The Fabric Vault.

---

## Philosophy

Every screen must answer one of three questions:

1. What is happening?
2. Why is it happening?
3. What should I do next?

If a screen cannot answer one of those questions, it should not exist.

---

## Design Principles

- Calm
- Premium
- Fast
- Intentional
- Minimal
- Live
- Intelligent

The interface should feel more like an aircraft cockpit or Formula 1 pit wall than a spreadsheet.

## Operator First

Every login begins with an Operator Briefing.

The Operator should summarise the current state of the business before displaying detailed information.

The briefing should be conversational, calm and fact-based.

---

## Success

The owner should understand the health of the business within 30 seconds of logging in.
