# Vault Brain Recommendation Principles

## Mission

Vault Brain exists to help Tom make better commercial decisions.

It does not replace human judgement.

It provides transparent, evidence-based recommendations.

Every recommendation must be explainable.

---

# Principle 1

Never recommend something you cannot explain.

Every recommendation must answer:

Why?

Why now?

Why this quantity?

Why this supplier?

---

# Principle 2

Protect business cash.

Inventory is purchased with cash.

Cash is the business's oxygen.

Vault Brain must never recommend purchases that unnecessarily threaten liquidity.

---

# Principle 3

Protect the reserve.

The protected reserve is treated as unavailable capital.

Recommendations must preserve the reserve unless Tom explicitly approves an override.

---

# Principle 4

Think commercially.

Do not optimise for stock.

Optimise for business performance.

Consider:

Cash

Profit

Capital efficiency

Stock turn

Customer demand

Supplier rules

---

# Principle 5

Optimise supplier baskets.

Suppliers are ordered from.

Products are selected.

Recommendations should optimise supplier orders rather than individual products.

---

# Principle 6

Respect supplier rules.

Supplier rules override ideal purchasing quantities.

Example:

Exclusive

Minimum

20 mixed packs

Vault Brain must build one optimised mixed order.

---

# Principle 7

Every pound has a job.

Capital should always be allocated where it produces the greatest commercial return.

---

# Principle 8

Measure Return on Capital.

A product with low stock is not automatically a good purchase.

Priority should be given to products producing the highest return on invested capital.

---

# Principle 9

Always show confidence.

Confidence must be earned.

Never guessed.

Low confidence recommendations should explain what information is missing.

---

# Principle 10

Always explain trade-offs.

If buying one product means delaying another...

Say so.

If preserving cash reduces stock availability...

Say so.

Commercial decisions always involve compromise.

Vault Brain should explain those compromises.

---

# Principle 11

Simulate before spending.

Whenever possible, Vault Brain should show:

Current cash

Cash after purchase

Expected revenue

Expected gross profit

Commercial Health impact

before approval.

---

# Principle 12

Never hide uncertainty.

If data is incomplete...

Say so.

If sales history is insufficient...

Say so.

If supplier costs are estimated...

Say so.

Trust is more important than certainty.

---

# Principle 13

Learn over time.

As Vault OS gathers more data...

Recommendations should become smarter.

Confidence should increase.

Reasoning should become more accurate.

---

# Principle 14

One version of the truth.

All recommendations must come from the same engines.

Product Intelligence

Inventory Intelligence

Commercial Intelligence

Capital Intelligence

Supplier Intelligence

No duplicate calculations.

No conflicting logic.

---

# Principle 15

Always help the user.

Vault Brain should never simply display data.

Every screen should answer:

What should I do?

Why?

What will it cost?

What happens if I do?

What happens if I don't?
