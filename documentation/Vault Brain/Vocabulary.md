# Vault Brain Vocabulary

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

Every word inside Vault OS has a specific meaning.

Words should never be interchangeable.

Consistent language creates a consistent product.

---

# Vault Brain

Vault Brain is the orchestration layer of Vault OS.

It does not generate data.

It understands data.

It decides what deserves attention.

---

# Event

An Event is something that happened.

Events are facts.

Examples

Order received

Catalogue uploaded

Stock adjusted

Supplier price changed

Shipment delivered

Customer review received

---

# Signal

A Signal is an observation created from one or more events.

Signals describe the current condition of the business.

Examples

Demand increasing

Inventory reducing

Cash improving

Supplier reliability falling

Signals never recommend actions.

---

# Insight

An Insight explains why a Signal matters.

Signals answer:

"What is happening?"

Insights answer:

"Why is this happening?"

Examples

Demand has increased because three influencers tagged the product.

Sales are slowing because inventory is fragmented across sizes.

---

# Impact

Impact describes the business consequence.

Examples

Projected stock-out

Lost revenue

Margin opportunity

Cash flow improvement

Risk reduction

---

# Mission

A Mission is a recommended piece of work.

Every Mission contains:

What happened

Why it matters

What should happen next

Estimated value

Confidence

Evidence

---

# Briefing

The Morning Briefing is the first experience every retailer should see.

It is not a dashboard.

It is a summary of what changed while the retailer was away.

---

# Signal Panel

Signal Panels display the current health of the business.

Signals support decisions.

They do not replace Missions.

---

# Knowledge Engine

Knowledge Engines specialise in understanding one area of the business.

Examples

Inventory Knowledge Engine

Commercial Knowledge Engine

Supplier Knowledge Engine

Catalogue Knowledge Engine

Knowledge Engines never make decisions.

---

# Decision Engine

Vault Brain is the Decision Engine.

It consumes knowledge from every engine.

It prioritises work.

It generates Missions.

---

# Cognitive Load

Cognitive Load is the amount of thinking required from the retailer.

Every feature should reduce cognitive load.

Never increase it.

---

# Automation

Automation removes administration.

Automation never removes ownership.

---

# Recommendation

A Recommendation is advice.

Recommendations always include evidence.

Recommendations never exist without confidence.

---

# Confidence

Confidence represents how certain Vault Brain is.

Every recommendation should explain why confidence is high or low.

---

# Business Health

Business Health represents the overall condition of the business.

It is calculated using multiple Signals.

It is not a manually entered metric.

---

# Workspace

A Workspace is an area dedicated to completing work.

Examples

Supplier Workspace

Catalogue Workspace

Inventory Workspace

Commercial Workspace

---

# Timeline

The Timeline records meaningful business activity.

It is a history of Events and completed Missions.

---

# Golden Rule

Events become Signals.

Signals become Insights.

Insights create Impact.

Impact generates Missions.

Missions drive Decisions.

Decisions grow the business.
